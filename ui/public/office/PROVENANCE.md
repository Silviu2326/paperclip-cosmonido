of a source pixel rectangle except the desk (source desk + laptop from the same set
stacked on the desk top) and the whiteboard (placeholder, drawn in code).

Notes:
- The bookshelf is a sideboard-style cabinet with items on top from city_inside; no
  acceptable tall bookshelf was found in the candidate sets, so it is a stand-in.
- The sofa, chair and lamp are the closest acceptable matches found in the sets; they are not exact LPC "office" pieces.
- Source sheets that were inspected but not used: `wood/furniture-v1.5.png` (opaque background, cannot be cut
  without a mask), `modern/`, `tiles/` misc atlas, `v_office.png` (annotated preview only).
- Generated by a local build script (not committed): compose rects, then `catalog.json` was written by hand to match the rects.

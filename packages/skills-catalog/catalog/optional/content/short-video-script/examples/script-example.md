# Short Video Script Example

## Brief

Audience: small teams that need to keep recurring work organized. Goal: show how a shared checklist reduces missed handoffs. Duration: about 20 seconds.

## Script

| Time | Spoken line | Visual beat | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | “Still asking who owns the next step?” | Open on an unanswered team message. | Who owns the next step? |
| 3–12s | “Put the task, owner, and due date in one shared checklist.” | Show a checklist item gaining an owner and date. | Task · owner · due date |
| 12–18s | “Now everyone can see what moves next.” | Show the next item marked ready. | Clear handoffs |
| 18–20s | “Try it with your next project.” | End on the shared checklist. | Start with one task |

This example avoids promising a measured business result and gives an editor recordable beats.

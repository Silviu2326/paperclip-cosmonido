# Content and Video

This team creates short-form video from an assignment brief, company context, and supplied media. The strategist turns the brief into a clear production plan; the editor works from the transcript and media studio; the copywriter prepares supporting copy; the reviewer checks the acceptance criteria and submits the finished deliverable for review.
